Author

Dr. Anshuman Sinha

Dr. Anshuman Sinha writes the Community Medicine notes on this site and is the developer of Stroma, the Community Medicine app published by Bottlegram Health. The notes are for MBBS students and for MD residents. They are not a copy of K. Park's textbook, and they are not a substitute for the edition a department has assigned.

He also teaches live sessions listed on the homepage, including the BCBR class with Dr. Kaushikee.

Questions about a note or about the app: anshuxinha@gmail.com.
