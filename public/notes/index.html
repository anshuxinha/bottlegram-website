Updated 8 October 2026. Written by Dr. Anshuman Sinha, Bottlegram Health.

These are community medicine notes for the MBBS prof and for MD residents. PSM notes and K Park notes are the names students search for. The pages below are the headings. The full chapters are in the app, which is not a scan of the textbook.
