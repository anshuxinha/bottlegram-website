Topic map

PSM notes for MBBS

Updated 8 October 2026. Written by Dr. Anshuman Sinha, Bottlegram Health.

These are the Community Medicine topics that show up as long questions, short notes, and vivas when the subject is taught from Park. Each block says what the answer is usually for. Rates, vaccine doses, and the book's tables stay in the current edition and in the official schedule.

Match this list to your own university papers before you treat it as complete. The study order is in how to study Community Medicine.

Concept of health and disease

Papers ask for a definition of health, the dimensions, and the difference between disease, illness, and sickness. Indicators of health are a separate short note. Write the definition first, then a classification, then one example of an indicator. Do not open with a history paragraph.

Epidemiology and screening

This is the chapter students are told not to skip. You need the uses of epidemiology, the study designs in one line each, what a rate differs from a ratio, and the idea of screening: who you test, and why a screening test is not a diagnostic test. Outbreak steps are a favourite long question. Learn them as a numbered sequence you can reproduce, then read the matching pages in Park so the sequence is not only a mnemonic.

Communicable and noncommunicable disease

Exams pick a disease and ask for epidemiological determinants, prevention, and the national programme attached to it. The mistake is a clinical case-sheet answer with no reservoir, no mode of transmission, and no programme. For noncommunicable disease, the same shape applies to risk factors and to the programme, not to a drug list from pharmacology.

National health programmes and immunisation

A programme answer needs the objective, the beneficiary, the strategy, and one recent change if you know it from a current source. The immunisation schedule is asked constantly. The ages and vaccines are on the National immunisation schedule page, taken from the Ministry's citizen note, with the link back to that note.

Demography and family planning

Demographic cycle, fertility indicators, and contraceptive methods by mechanism are the usual set. A method answer needs mechanism, advantages, and contraindications in headings. Eligible-couple and couple-protection ideas come up as short notes. If you quote a fertility rate, take it from the current National Family Health Survey at rchiips.org/nfhs. Stroma has an NFHS-5 and NFHS-6 comparison in the toolbox.

Maternal and child health

Antenatal care, postnatal care, indicators such as maternal mortality and infant mortality, and the programmes around them are standard long questions. Define the indicator, give the current official figure only if you have checked it, and name the services rather than a story of one clinic.

Nutrition

Classification of foods, protein-energy malnutrition, micronutrients, and the programmes (including mid-day meal and iodine) are short-note material. Assessment of nutritional status, including growth charts, appears in practicals as often as in theory. Learn the categories and the cut-offs from Park, and check any national figure against the survey year printed next to it.

Environment, occupational health, and disasters

Water, waste, housing, vectors, and air are environmental long questions. Occupational health asks for a named disease, the exposure, and prevention, plus ESI and factories-act ideas as short notes. Disaster management is usually a cycle: preparedness, response, recovery. These chapters are long in Park and repetitive in papers, which is why notes help, and why the book is still the place to confirm a standard.

Biostatistics

Mean, median, mode, a normal curve, sampling, sensitivity, specificity, and predictive value are the calculation set. Tests of significance are where students stall. Do a worked example until the choice of test is boring. A phone calculator is useful here. Stroma's store listing includes biostatistics tools for that reason. The interpretation of a result still has to be yours.

Health care of the community

Primary health care, the levels of care in India, and the staff and services at a sub-centre, primary health centre, and community health centre are viva and short-note staples. Health and wellness centres get asked because the delivery system changed. If your edition is old, confirm the current facility names before you write them.

Short notes and long answers for several of these chapters are in the app. How that relates to the textbook is in Park textbook notes.
