Sources of data

Health information

8 October 2026. Dr. Anshuman Sinha.

A health information system is the way a service finds out what is happening to a population, in time to do something about it. Community Medicine questions ask you to name the source, say what it can measure, and say what it cannot.

Which sources are used in India?

  • Census. A count of the whole population, once a decade. It is the denominator for many rates, and it is already old by the time the next health survey is published.
  • Civil registration. Births and deaths as they are registered. Completeness varies. A rate built on incomplete registration undercounts the events that were never written down.
  • Sample Registration System. A continuous sample of the population, used for birth rates, death rates, and infant mortality where registration is not complete enough to stand alone.
  • National Family Health Survey. A household survey of a sample. Fertility, mortality, nutrition, and the use of services are what students quote from it. The figures belong to a named round. The official site is rchiips.org/nfhs.
  • Health management information system. The routine reports from facilities: sessions held, doses given, cases seen. It describes the work of the service. It does not, by itself, describe the people who never reached a facility.
  • Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme. Weekly reporting of selected conditions, so that an outbreak can be seen early. It is not a census of every disease.

What is the usual mistake?

Treating a facility report as if it were a population rate. Immunisation coverage from a session register and immunisation coverage from a household survey answer different questions. The first says what the clinic recorded. The second says what a sample of households reported. Both can be useful. They are not interchangeable.

The same caution applies to a programme target. Sustainable Development Goal 3 states a target. A survey states an estimate. An answer that merges them into one number has lost the source.
