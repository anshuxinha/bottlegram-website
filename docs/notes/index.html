MBBS and MD

Guides for MBBS and MD Community Medicine

Updated 8 October 2026. Written by Dr. Anshuman Sinha, Bottlegram Health.

The first notes are things you can revise from: definitions, a worked Pearl index, and a screening table. The later notes are about how MBBS students and MD residents prepare, and where Park, the review books, Marrow, and Stroma each fit.

  • Concept of health and disease

    The WHO definition, disease versus illness versus sickness, the four concepts of health, the triad, the iceberg, and the levels of prevention. Written so you can turn it into a long answer.

  • Pearl index, with a worked example

    What the index measures, the formula, why a longer study looks safer than it is, and a practice calculation you can check by hand.

  • Sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV

    A 2-by-2 table with numbers, and why a good test still has a weak positive predictive value when the disease is uncommon.

  • Park, Marrow, review books, and Stroma

    What each option is actually for, for an MBBS prof and for an MD resident, and where Stroma is the wrong tool.

  • Park textbook notes: a shorter pass for MBBS

    Who should keep reading Park, who the short notes are for, and how Stroma compares with a printed edition and with circulating PDFs.

  • How to study Community Medicine in MBBS

    A revision order for the prof: which kinds of chapters to open first, how to turn a Park paragraph into an answer, and which numbers you should not memorise from an old photocopy.

  • PSM topics MBBS exams keep asking

    The topic list students actually revise: health and disease, epidemiology, programmes, nutrition, environment, maternal and child health, demography, and biostatistics.
