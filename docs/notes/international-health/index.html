WHO and the IHR

International health

8 October 2026. Dr. Anshuman Sinha.

International health, in this chapter, is the work that crosses a border: an outbreak that can travel, a standard that more than one country has agreed to, and the agencies that carry it. It is not a list of logos.

What is the World Health Organization?

The World Health Organization is the specialised health agency of the United Nations. Its constitution, which came into force in 1948, is also the source of the definition of health used in concept of health and disease: complete physical, mental, and social well-being, not merely the absence of disease. The organisation works through a headquarters and regional offices. India sits in the South-East Asia Region.

The work students are asked to name includes technical cooperation with countries, the setting of norms and standards, and the response to epidemics. A World Health Report is a publication of that organisation. It is not itself a regulation.

What do the International Health Regulations require?

The International Health Regulations (2005) are a legal instrument binding on the states that are party to them. Their purpose is to prevent and respond to the international spread of disease, while avoiding unnecessary interference with travel and trade. A country is expected to detect and report certain events, and to build the capacity to do that. The Director-General can declare a public health emergency of international concern. The regulations are not a promise that every outbreak will be declared one.

Which other agencies come up in the same answer?

  • UNICEF works for children. Immunisation, nutrition, and education are the usual examples in a Community Medicine answer.
  • A bilateral agency is one government helping another. It is not the same thing as a United Nations body.
  • A philanthropic foundation can fund a programme without being either of those. Name it as what it is.

Quarantine, the older word, was a measure at the border. The Regulations are the current frame for that kind of decision. The surveillance that feeds a report is discussed with health information.
