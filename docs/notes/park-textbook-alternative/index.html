K Park notes

Updated 8 October 2026. Written by Dr. Anshuman Sinha, Bottlegram Health.

Park textbook notes are what MBBS students search for when K. Park's Textbook of Preventive and Social Medicine is the assigned book and the prof is closer than the page count. Stroma is a Community Medicine app for that job: short notes, long-answer questions, and exam tips on the phone. It is not a photocopy of Park.

What students are actually trying to solve

In Indian medical colleges, Community Medicine (PSM) is still taught from Park. On forums such as r/indianmedschool, the repeated complaint is the same: the book reads as long paragraphs, and a 10-mark answer needs headings. Students then hunt for a shorter set: Vivek Jain's review book, Arpit PSM notes, Marrow notes, college compilations, or a PDF of an older edition.

Those shorter sets have a real use. They also go stale. National Family Health Survey figures, programme names, and the immunisation schedule move. A PDF saved before an edition change does not.

How do Park, a notes file, and Stroma differ?

Who should keep reading Park

Read Park if your university answers are marked against it, if a teacher has marked chapters, or if you want the argument around a definition and not only the line you will write. A distinction in many colleges still comes from knowing the book, not from owning a shorter file. Students who scored well often say the notes told them which pages of Park were worth the time. That order is sound: notes first, then the chapters that carry marks.

Who is Stroma for?

Stroma is for an MBBS student, and for a Community Medicine resident, who wants one library on the phone. It has the Community Medicine notes, guideline updates, biostatistics calculators, and a resident course. It does not reprint Park.

Questions students ask

Is there an easy alternative to Park for the university exam?

There is an easier revision path. There is not an official substitute if the department set Park. Use short notes to choose chapters, then read those chapters in the book. The topic list is on PSM topics MBBS exams keep asking, and the order of work is in how to study Community Medicine.

Should I download a Park PDF?

No. A full-text PDF of a current textbook shared on document sites is someone else's copyright. It is also the copy most likely to be an old edition with old survey numbers.

Is Stroma a replacement for Park?

No. Keep the book your college assigned. Use Stroma when you need the short pass, the exam headings, or a figure that the printed edition no longer matches.
