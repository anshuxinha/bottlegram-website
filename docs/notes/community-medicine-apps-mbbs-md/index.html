MBBS and MD

Park, Marrow, review books, and Stroma

Updated 8 October 2026. Written by Dr. Anshuman Sinha, from public descriptions of each product and from the India App Store listing for Stroma checked the same day. This is not a feature-by-feature audit of Marrow or PrepLadder.

Community Medicine students are not choosing one app to replace a career of reading. An MBBS student is choosing how to turn Park into a prof answer. An MD resident is choosing how to teach that subject, sit a university exam, and keep survey numbers current. The products below solve different halves of that. Pretending one of them does both is how the marketing pages in this category waste your evening.

What people are actually buying

On r/indianmedschool, the recurring MBBS complaint is that Park's Textbook of Preventive and Social Medicine is too long to convert into headings in the last fortnight. The substitutes named in those threads are Vivek Jain's review book, Arpit's PSM notes, Marrow notes, college compilations, and sometimes Suryakantha. For NEET PG, the same students talk about Marrow, PrepLadder, and short revision notes, with PSM treated as one subject among many. Separate from all of that, MD Community Medicine has its own reading: national programmes, research methods, and teaching, which a 19-subject entrance app only samples.

MBBS, in practice

Keep the edition of Park your college named. Use one shorter set to choose chapters and to draft headings. Stroma is that shorter set if you want it on a phone and you want the notes updated between editions. Vivek Jain or a finished college notebook is that shorter set if you work on paper. Marrow is a reasonable PSM pass if you are already paying for it for the entrance exam. Adding all four in the last month is how the subject becomes longer, not shorter.

The undergraduate method, including which chapters carry long questions, is in how to study Community Medicine. A direct Park comparison is in Park textbook notes.

MD, in practice

An entrance-app notes file will not carry a residency. You still need programme updates, a way to check NFHS figures, and material you can teach from. Stroma's resident course is the part of the app aimed at that. It does not remove the need to read the original orders and survey reports. The teaching pages on this site, such as the Pearl index calculation and the screening table, are the same kind of thing the library is for: a worked answer, not a coaching slogan.

Questions that come up

Does an MD resident still need Park?

For the parts of the syllabus and the teaching that still cite it, yes. For daily revision, a shorter file is the tool you will actually open. Stroma does not claim to retire the departmental copies.

Is Stroma a Marrow alternative?

No. Marrow covers the entrance exam across subjects. Stroma covers Community Medicine for MBBS and for MD. If you need surgery questions, Stroma will not help you.

Which failure should you believe?

Stroma is iPhone and iPad only on the current listing, so an Android-only student should not plan around it. It also does not publish a chapter-by-chapter map against the 28th edition of Park. Several chapters have short notes and long answers. "Several" is the honest word.
