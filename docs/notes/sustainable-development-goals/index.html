SDG 3

Sustainable development goals

8 October 2026. Dr. Anshuman Sinha.

The Sustainable Development Goals are 17 goals adopted by the United Nations in 2015, with a horizon of 2030. They replaced the Millennium Development Goals. In Community Medicine the one that is asked as a long question is Goal 3, good health and well-being. The others come in when a determinant sits outside the health ministry: poverty, hunger, water, education, and inequality.

What does Goal 3 ask for?

The official list is on sdgs.un.org/goals/goal3. The targets, in the words an answer can use, are:

  • Maternal mortality reduced to less than 70 per 100,000 live births.
  • An end to preventable deaths of newborns and children under 5, with neonatal mortality at least as low as 12 per 1,000 live births and under-5 mortality at least as low as 25 per 1,000.
  • An end to the epidemics of AIDS, tuberculosis, malaria, and neglected tropical diseases, and a fight against hepatitis, waterborne disease, and other communicable diseases.
  • A reduction, by one third, of premature mortality from noncommunicable disease, and promotion of mental health.
  • Prevention and treatment of substance abuse.
  • Halving global deaths and injuries from road traffic.
  • Universal access to sexual and reproductive health care.
  • Universal health coverage, including financial risk protection and access to essential medicines and vaccines.
  • A substantial reduction in deaths and illness from hazardous chemicals, and from air, water, and soil pollution.
  • The tobacco convention, research and vaccines, the health workforce, and capacity to manage health emergencies. These are the means-of-implementation targets, 3.a to 3.d.

India's progress on maternal and child mortality is reported against these numbers. Quote an Indian figure only from the survey that measured it, such as the National Family Health Survey, and name the round. The target and the latest Indian estimate are not the same sentence.

How do the goals differ from the Millennium Development Goals?

The Millennium Development Goals were narrower. Three of them were explicitly about health: child mortality, maternal health, and AIDS, malaria, and other diseases. The Sustainable Development Goals keep those concerns and add noncommunicable disease, injuries, universal health coverage, and the causes that sit outside a hospital. A short note that only says "there are 17 goals" has not answered a question about Goal 3.
